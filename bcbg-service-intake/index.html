Bucks County Bike Garage · Service Workflow

Service Intake — Product Information Architecture

A designer-facing map of the mechanic's journey through a repair. The primary object is a customer-owned bicycle moving through an agreement about what work should be done, the work itself, and a safe handoff back to the customer.

01

Intake

  • Customer identity
  • Bike details
  • Requested service
  • Intake photos
  • Initial authorization
02

Inspection

  • Mechanic evaluates bike
  • Findings documented
  • Existing service reviewed
  • Additional work proposed
03

Customer Decision

  • Finding communicated
  • Proposal snapshot
  • Approved or declined
04

Authorized Work

  • Intake-authorized work
  • Approved additional work
  • Service lines = work commitment
  • Mechanic completes work
05

Final QC

  • Final once-over
  • Test ride / Not required
  • Finished-bike photo
06

Ready for Customer

  • Customer handoff
  • Completed-work summary
  • Customer communication
07

Waiting for Pickup

  • Customer notified
  • Bike remains in shop custody
  • Pickup / delivery obligation
08

Payment → Closed

  • Payment recorded
  • Bike handed back
  • Repair history retained
At every stage: Where is the bicycle? Who owes the next action? What must happen before the job advances?

Questions for design critique

  • Does this lifecycle match the mechanic's mental model, or expose too much process?
  • What should remain continuously visible as the mechanic moves between stages?
  • Are Finding, Customer Decision, and Service Line understandable concepts—or implementation concepts leaking into the product?
  • Should the interface orient primarily around the physical bike, the customer, or the service job?
  • Which transitions need explicit confirmation, and which should feel nearly invisible?
  • What should be immediately obvious when reopening a bike after several days?

Product IA only. Technical architecture and the working application repository are intentionally omitted. No customer or private operational data is included.
