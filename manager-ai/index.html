Emerging product / operating thesis

Manager AI

An AI-powered general-manager layer for a small business: not another system the owner has to operate, but a layer that understands the state of the systems and helps the owner run the business.

What if the owner didn't have to constantly operate every system directly?
Manager layer

Understand the state of the business and surface what matters now

  • What needs attention today?
  • Which customers are waiting on us?
  • What did we promise that hasn't happened?
  • What is blocked, overdue, unassigned, or likely to fall through the cracks?
  • Which decisions actually require the owner's judgment?

Customer & sales

CRM, test rides, follow-up, orders, commitments, communications.

Operations

Service workflow, fulfillment, inventory, receiving, assembly, delivery.

Systems of record

POS, CRM, waiver systems, service data, inventory and other trusted operational sources.

Why this is emerging

Built from operating experience, not an AI feature hunt

The idea emerged from hands-on work inside small bicycle businesses: cleaning up operational layers, connecting systems, representing customer obligations, and seeing how much context still lives in a person's head. The premise is that the AI layer is only useful when the operational layers underneath it are trustworthy.

Questions worth pressure-testing

  • Is “manager” the right abstraction, or should the product be narrower?
  • Which decisions should AI surface versus make?
  • What minimum set of connected systems creates meaningful value?
  • Can this work across small retail businesses without erasing how differently they operate?
