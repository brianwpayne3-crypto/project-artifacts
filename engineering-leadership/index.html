How I tend to lead

I am most useful where the problem crosses boundaries: product and engineering, architecture and delivery, customer behavior and system design, or organizational structure and technical ownership. I tend to make those boundaries explicit, reduce unnecessary complexity, and give teams clearer ownership of outcomes.

The current projects elsewhere in this collection show that approach at a much smaller scale, where I can work directly inside the operation and carry the problem from observation through product and system design.
