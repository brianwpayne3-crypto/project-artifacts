Recent work applies the same systems thinking at a different scale: understanding workflows, defining ownership boundaries, simplifying operations, integrating SaaS systems and building narrow software only where it earns its complexity.

The thread through the work

The scale changes—from consumer travel and sports platforms to a bicycle shop—but the recurring problem is similar: understand the job people are trying to accomplish, make system boundaries explicit, reduce unnecessary cognitive load, and build organizations/software around outcomes rather than around the technology itself.
