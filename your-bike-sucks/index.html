Deliberately tiny creative experiment

YourBikeSucks.com

A satire of bicycle seriousness expressed through the Bureau of Bicycle Assessment: absurd officialdom applied to the deeply consequential matter of deciding whether a bicycle sucks.

Constraint as product strategy

V0 is intentionally minimal: a strong visual identity, one joke, restrained copy, and no speculative infrastructure.

Possible V1

Click to receive an official adjudication with a globally incremental case/reference number, reinforcing the bureaucratic fiction.

Physical artifact

Exploring dropshipped inspection stickers, ideally with unique real case numbers corresponding to the digital assessment.

Why keep it small?

It is a useful experiment in brand, product restraint, visual language, and making something memorable without needing to manufacture a large business case.
